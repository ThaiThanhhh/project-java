PICKLEBALL COURT – LAYOUT AND DIMENSIONS

A pickleball court has similar dimensions to a doubles badminton court, measuring 13.4 meters (44 feet) long and 6.1 meters (20 feet) wide. Interestingly, this court is used for both singles and doubles play. The net stands at a height of 91.4 cm (36 inches) at the sidelines and 86.4 cm (34 inches) in the middle.

The court is marked similarly to a tennis court, with right and left service areas, along with a 2.1-meter (7-foot) non-volley zone in front of the net, often called the “kitchen.” You can build your own pickleball court or repurpose existing tennis or badminton courts for conversion.

HISTORY OF PICKLEBALL

Have you ever wondered about the origins of the dynamic sport of Pickleball? Let's explore the interesting story of the birth of this unique sport.

On a summer day in 1965, on the beautiful Bainbridge Island – just a short ferry ride from Seattle, Washington, three fathers Joel Pritchard, Bill Bell and Barney McCallum created Pickleball. Their motivation? Simply wanting to find a new recreational activity for children who were bored with the usual summer games.
